<section class="app-tab-panel is-active">

  <p><strong>Some Russian services may not work when you are connected through a foreign IP address.</strong></p>

  <p>For example, restrictions may affect Avito, Gosuslugi, Kinopoisk, and other Russian websites and apps.</p>

  <div class="guide-note">
    <strong>What to do</strong>
    <p>Change your Slon Privacy location and try opening the service again.</p>
  </div>

  <section class="guide-step">
    <div class="guide-step-number">1</div>
    <div class="guide-step-content">
      <h2>Choose the right location</h2>
      <p>For Russian services, we recommend using Slon Privacy locations where all or most Russian websites and apps are available.</p>
      <p>Try the <strong>RU → ND</strong> location if you need access to both Russian and international services. If you are abroad and need access to Russian services, use the <strong>RU</strong> location.</p>
      <p><a href="{{ site.baseurl }}/en/knowledge/about/locations/">View the list of Slon Privacy locations →</a></p>
    </div>
  </section>

  <section class="guide-step">
    <div class="guide-step-number">2</div>
    <div class="guide-step-content">
      <h2>Reconnect</h2>
      <p>Disconnect your current VPN connection, select a new location, and connect again. Then reopen the website or app you need.</p>
    </div>
  </section>

  <div class="guide-success">
    <strong>Done!</strong> If the service still does not open, try another suitable Slon Privacy location.
  </div>

</section>
