<section class="app-tab-panel is-active">

  <p><strong>If ChatGPT, OpenAI, Grok, or another international service does not open while using Slon Privacy, changing your connection location is usually enough to solve the problem.</strong></p>

  <section class="guide-step">
    <div class="guide-step-number">1</div>
    <div class="guide-step-content">
      <h2>Change your location</h2>
      <p>Disconnect from your current location and connect to another international Slon Privacy location. Some services may restrict access from certain IP addresses or countries.</p>
      <p><a href="{{ site.baseurl }}/en/knowledge/about/locations/">View available Slon Privacy locations →</a></p>
    </div>
  </section>

  <section class="guide-step">
    <div class="guide-step-number">2</div>
    <div class="guide-step-content">
      <h2>Try several locations</h2>
      <p>If the service is still unavailable, try a few other international locations. Availability of a particular service may vary depending on the server you select.</p>
    </div>
  </section>

  <section class="guide-step">
    <div class="guide-step-number">3</div>
    <div class="guide-step-content">
      <h2>Try incognito mode</h2>
      <p>If ChatGPT or another service still does not open, try opening it in an incognito or private browser window. Saved cookies and data from previous sessions can sometimes interfere with the service after you change locations.</p>
    </div>
  </section>

  <div class="guide-warning">
    <strong>If nothing helped</strong>
    <p>Contact Slon Privacy support. Tell us which service does not open and which locations you have already tried — we will do our best to help.</p>
  </div>

</section>
