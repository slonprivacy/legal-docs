<section class="app-tab-panel is-active">

  <p><strong>If a particular website does not open while Slon Privacy is connected, it does not always mean there is a problem with your connection. Some websites restrict access through VPNs or for users from certain countries.</strong></p>

  <div class="guide-note">
    <strong>Why this happens</strong>
    <p>Some websites may detect VPN use through indirect signals — for example, a mismatch between your device's time zone and the selected location, or browser fingerprint data. A website may also restrict access for users from certain countries.</p>
  </div>

  <section class="guide-step">
    <div class="guide-step-number">1</div>
    <div class="guide-step-content">
      <h2>Change your location</h2>
      <p>First, select a server in the same or a similar time zone to yours. If that does not help, try another location. <a href="{{ site.baseurl }}/en/knowledge/about/locations/">Learn more about available Slon Privacy locations →</a></p>
    </div>
  </section>

  <section class="guide-step">
    <div class="guide-step-number">2</div>
    <div class="guide-step-content">
      <h2>Use a different VPN protocol</h2>
      <p>In the Happ or INCY settings, select another available protocol and try connecting again.</p>
    </div>
  </section>

  <section class="guide-step">
    <div class="guide-step-number">3</div>
    <div class="guide-step-content">
      <h2>Open the website in incognito mode</h2>
      <p>This can help determine whether the problem is related to cookies, cache, or saved website data.</p>
    </div>
  </section>

  <section class="guide-step">
    <div class="guide-step-number">4</div>
    <div class="guide-step-content">
      <h2>Limit browser tracking</h2>
      <p>You can use extensions that block trackers and reduce the amount of data websites can use to build a browser fingerprint.</p>
    </div>
  </section>

  <div class="guide-warning">
    <strong>If nothing helped</strong>
    <p>Contact Slon Privacy support. Tell us which website does not open and which location you are using — we will do our best to help.</p>
  </div>

</section>
