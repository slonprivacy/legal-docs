<section class="app-tab-panel is-active">
  <p><strong>You can use Slon Privacy on your computer, phone, and tablet, and set it up on devices used by people close to you. One subscription supports up to 3 devices.</strong></p>

  <div class="guide-note">
    <strong>Two ways to connect</strong>
    <p>You can sign in to your account on another device or securely share your subscription using a QR code — without sharing your password or access to your profile.</p>
  </div>

  <section class="guide-step">
    <div class="guide-step-number">1</div>
    <div class="guide-step-content">
      <h2>Sign in to your account on another device</h2>
      <p>This is the easiest option. On the new device, sign in to your Slon Privacy account using Telegram or Email, then install the app and add your subscription using the guide for your platform:</p>
      <ul class="guide-simple-list">
        <li><a href="{{ site.baseurl }}/en/knowledge/install/android/">Android</a></li>
        <li><a href="{{ site.baseurl }}/en/knowledge/install/ios/">iOS</a></li>
        <li><a href="{{ site.baseurl }}/en/knowledge/install/windows/">Windows</a></li>
        <li><a href="{{ site.baseurl }}/en/knowledge/install/macos/">macOS</a></li>
      </ul>
      <div class="guide-warning"><strong>Important:</strong> signing in to your Slon Privacy account gives access to your profile and settings. Use this method only on your own devices or on devices belonging to people you fully trust.</div>
    </div>
  </section>

  <section class="guide-step">
    <div class="guide-step-number">2</div>
    <div class="guide-step-content">
      <h2>Share your subscription using a QR code</h2>
      <p>This method lets you connect another device without giving access to your account.</p>
      <div class="guide-warning"><strong>Before sending the QR code:</strong> make sure the app you select in your account — <strong>Happ</strong> or <strong>INCY</strong> — is already installed on the other device. The QR code must be created specifically for the installed app.</div>
      <ul class="guide-simple-list">
        <li>Open your Slon Privacy account on your device.</li>
        <li>Go to the option for connecting a new device.</li>
        <li>Select the required platform and the app installed on the other device — Happ or INCY.</li>
        <li>Click the <strong>subscription QR code</strong> button.</li>
        <li>Save the QR code and send the image to the other device using any convenient method.</li>
      </ul>
      <img class="guide-image guide-image-small" src="{{ site.baseurl }}/assets/img/knowledge-base/multi-device/01-subscription-qr.jpg" alt="Subscription QR code button on the Slon Privacy setup page" loading="lazy">
    </div>
  </section>

  <section class="guide-step">
    <div class="guide-step-number">3</div>
    <div class="guide-step-content">
      <h2>Add the QR code to Happ</h2>
      <p>Save the QR code you received to your Gallery. Open <strong>Happ</strong> and tap:</p>
      <p><strong>“+” → “QR Code” → “Gallery” → select the saved QR code.</strong></p>
      <p>Your Slon Privacy subscription will be added automatically.</p>
      <section class="guide-video-section" aria-labelledby="happ-qr-video-title">
        <h2 id="happ-qr-video-title">Video guide for Happ</h2>
        <p>Adding a Slon Privacy subscription from a saved QR code.</p>
        <video class="guide-video" controls playsinline preload="metadata">
          <source src="{{ site.baseurl }}/assets/video/knowledge-base/multi-device/happ-qr-import.mp4" type="video/mp4">
          Your browser does not support video playback.
        </video>
      </section>
    </div>
  </section>

  <section class="guide-step">
    <div class="guide-step-number">4</div>
    <div class="guide-step-content">
      <h2>Add the QR code to INCY</h2>
      <p>Save the QR code you received to your Gallery. Open <strong>INCY</strong> and tap:</p>
      <p><strong>QR code icon → “Choose from Gallery” → select the saved QR code.</strong></p>
      <p>Your Slon Privacy subscription will be added automatically.</p>
      <section class="guide-video-section" aria-labelledby="incy-qr-video-title">
        <h2 id="incy-qr-video-title">Video guide for INCY</h2>
        <p>Adding a Slon Privacy subscription from a saved QR code.</p>
        <video class="guide-video" controls playsinline preload="metadata">
          <source src="{{ site.baseurl }}/assets/video/knowledge-base/multi-device/incy-qr-import.mp4" type="video/mp4">
          Your browser does not support video playback.
        </video>
      </section>
    </div>
  </section>

  <div class="guide-success">
    <strong>Done!</strong> After adding the subscription, select a server and connect to Slon Privacy. The QR code lets you connect an additional device without sharing your password or access to your account.
  </div>
</section>
