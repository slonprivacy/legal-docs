<p>Some apps may be unavailable in the Russian App Store. This includes apps that can be used to connect to <strong>Slon Privacy</strong>.</p>

<div class="guide-warning">
  <strong>Important:</strong> malicious actors may use similar app names and icons. We recommend installing Happ and INCY through your <strong>Slon Privacy</strong> account or carefully checking the developer before installation. In the App Store, the developer of <strong>Happ</strong> is <strong>Flyfrog LLC</strong>, and the developer of <strong>INCY</strong> is <strong>LLC ITDEV</strong>.
</div>

<p>If the app you need does not appear in App Store search or its page says that it is unavailable in your region, there are two ways to solve the problem:</p>

<ul class="guide-simple-list">
  <li><strong>Change your App Store region.</strong> You continue using your current Apple Account but gain access to apps from another region.</li>
  <li><strong>Create an additional Apple Account.</strong> This is a backup option if you cannot change the region of your current account or do not want to change its settings.</li>
</ul>

<div class="guide-note">
  <strong>What we recommend</strong>
  <p>Since April 2026, changing the App Store region has become easier for some Russian accounts. In some cases, the region can be changed without adding a bank card and without canceling some active subscriptions.</p>
  <p>We therefore recommend <strong>trying to change the region of your current account first</strong> — this is usually easier than creating an additional Apple Account.</p>
  <p><strong>Important:</strong> requirements may vary depending on the status of your account. Apple may require you to spend your remaining balance, cancel certain subscriptions, change Family Sharing settings, or add a payment method.</p>
  <p>If you cannot change the region, use the second option — <strong>create an additional Apple Account</strong>.</p>
</div>

<div class="guide-note">
  <strong>If the app is unavailable in the Russian App Store</strong>
  <p>App availability depends on the country or region of your Apple Account. To download an app that is not available in the Russian App Store, you can change the region of your current account or create an additional foreign Apple Account.</p>
</div>

  <section id="change-region-panel" class="app-tab-panel is-active" role="tabpanel">
    <h2>Change App Store Region</h2>
    <p><strong>Changing your region gives you access to apps from another App Store while continuing to use your current Apple Account.</strong></p>

    <div class="guide-note">
      <strong>Before you start</strong>
      <p>Check the remaining balance on your Apple Account. Any remaining balance must be spent before changing the region.</p>
      <p>Open <strong>App Store → profile</strong> and check your account balance.</p>
      <p>If the remaining amount is too small to make a purchase, contact Apple Support.</p>
      <p>Also check your active subscriptions, Family Sharing membership, and available payment methods.</p>
    </div>

    <div class="guide-warning">
      <strong>Important:</strong> after changing your region, some apps installed from the Russian App Store may become unavailable for redownload or updates if they are not available in the new region. If necessary, you can temporarily switch back to your previous region.
    </div>

    <p>In some cases, Apple allows you to change the region without canceling active subscriptions or adding a bank card. We therefore recommend trying the steps below first.</p>
    <p>If Apple does not allow you to continue, the system will usually indicate what is preventing the region change.</p>

    <section class="guide-step">
      <div class="guide-step-number">1</div>
      <div class="guide-step-content">
        <h2>Open your account settings</h2>
        <p>Open the <strong>App Store</strong> on your iPhone or iPad.</p>
        <p>Tap your profile icon, then tap your account name.</p>
        <p>If necessary, authenticate with Face ID, Touch ID, or your password.</p>
      </div>
    </section>

    <section class="guide-step">
      <div class="guide-step-number">2</div>
      <div class="guide-step-content">
        <h2>Open the region settings</h2>
        <p>Go to:</p>
        <p><strong>“Country/Region” → “Change Country or Region”.</strong></p>
      </div>
    </section>

    <section class="guide-step">
      <div class="guide-step-number">3</div>
      <div class="guide-step-content">
        <h2>Choose a new region</h2>
        <p>Select the country whose App Store you want to use. Most importantly, make sure the app you need is available in that region.</p>
        <p>You can consider:</p>
        <ul class="guide-simple-list">
          <li><strong>Kazakhstan</strong> — a convenient option for Russian-speaking users.</li>
          <li><strong>Türkiye</strong> — many international apps are available.</li>
          <li><strong>United States</strong> — one of the most extensive App Store catalogs.</li>
          <li><strong>Germany and other EU countries</strong> — also suitable for accessing most international apps.</li>
        </ul>
        <div class="guide-warning">
          <strong>Note:</strong> if you plan to make purchases or pay for subscriptions through the App Store, available payment methods will depend on the country you select.
        </div>
      </div>
    </section>

    <section class="guide-step">
      <div class="guide-step-number">4</div>
      <div class="guide-step-content">
        <h2>Accept Apple's terms</h2>
        <p>Review the terms and conditions for the selected region and confirm your agreement.</p>
      </div>
    </section>

    <section class="guide-step">
      <div class="guide-step-number">5</div>
      <div class="guide-step-content">
        <h2>Enter the required information</h2>
        <p>Apple will ask you to provide information for the selected region.</p>
        <p>If the <strong>None</strong> option is available, you can select it instead of adding a bank card.</p>
        <p>Complete the remaining required fields. Use valid address information for the selected country and region.</p>
        <div class="guide-warning">
          <strong>Important:</strong> the information required depends on the selected region and the status of your Apple Account.
        </div>
      </div>
    </section>

    <section class="guide-step">
      <div class="guide-step-number">6</div>
      <div class="guide-step-content">
        <h2>Complete the region change</h2>
        <p>Tap <strong>“Done”</strong> and wait for the App Store to update.</p>
        <p>If the changes do not apply immediately, completely close and reopen the App Store. Restart your device if necessary.</p>
        <p>Then find the app you need and install it as usual.</p>
      </div>
    </section>

    <div class="guide-success">
      <strong>Done!</strong> The App Store now uses the selected region. After installing Happ or INCY, you can continue setting up <strong>Slon Privacy</strong>.
    </div>

    <div class="guide-note">
      <strong>Continue installation</strong>
      <p>
        <a href="{{ site.baseurl }}/en/knowledge/install/ios/">
          Return to the Slon Privacy iOS installation guide →
        </a>
      </p>
    </div>

    <section class="guide-faq" aria-labelledby="change-region-faq-title">
      <h2 id="change-region-faq-title">Troubleshooting</h2>
      <details>
        <summary>Apple does not allow me to change the region</summary>
        <p>Check the reason shown by the system. A remaining account balance, certain subscriptions, or other account restrictions may prevent you from changing the region.</p>
      </details>
      <details>
        <summary>There is no “None” option for the payment method</summary>
        <p>Whether you can continue without a payment method depends on the selected region and the status of your account. In some cases, Apple may require payment information.</p>
      </details>
      <details>
        <summary>Family Sharing prevents me from changing the region</summary>
        <p>For some accounts, participation in a Family Sharing group may prevent a region change. Follow the message Apple displays for your account.</p>
      </details>
      <details>
        <summary>The app does not appear after changing the region</summary>
        <p>Completely close the App Store and open it again. Restart your device if necessary.</p>
        <p>Also make sure the app you need is actually available in the selected region.</p>
      </details>
      <details>
        <summary>I cannot change the region</summary>
        <p>Use the second option — <button type="button" class="guide-inline-tab-link" data-open-app-tab="new-account-panel"><strong>create an additional Apple Account</strong></button>.</p>
      </details>
    </section>
  </section>

  <section id="new-account-panel" class="app-tab-panel" role="tabpanel">
    <h2>Create a New Apple Account</h2>
    <p><strong>If you cannot change the region of your main account, you can create an additional Apple Account for another region.</strong></p>
    <p>You can use it only for the <strong>App Store</strong> — to install and update the apps you need. You do not need to change your main iCloud account with your photos, contacts, backups, and other data.</p>

    <div class="guide-note">
      <strong>Before you start</strong>
      <p>Prepare:</p>
      <ul class="guide-simple-list">
        <li>a separate email address that has not previously been used for an Apple Account;</li>
        <li>a permanent phone number for registration verification that you will continue to have access to;</li>
        <li>the country or region for the new account.</li>
      </ul>
      <p>Choose a region where the app you need is available.</p>
    </div>

    <section class="guide-step">
      <div class="guide-step-number">1</div>
      <div class="guide-step-content">
        <h2>Create a new Apple Account</h2>
        <p>Go to Apple's official account creation page.</p>
        <p>You can use your browser's private or incognito mode when registering the additional account.</p>
      </div>
    </section>

    <section class="guide-step">
      <div class="guide-step-number">2</div>
      <div class="guide-step-content">
        <h2>Enter your information</h2>
        <p>Provide:</p>
        <ul class="guide-simple-list">
          <li>your first and last name;</li>
          <li>date of birth;</li>
          <li>the required country or region;</li>
          <li>a new email address;</li>
          <li>a strong password;</li>
          <li>a phone number.</li>
        </ul>
        <p>If you plan to pay for apps or subscriptions using the new account, check the available payment methods for the selected country in advance.</p>
        <p>The country of your phone number does not necessarily have to match the account region. In some cases, a Russian phone number with the <strong>+7</strong> country code can be used for an account in another region.</p>
        <div class="guide-warning">
          <strong>If Apple does not accept a +7 number:</strong> use another permanent phone number that you will continue to have access to. You may need this number later to verify your identity or recover access to your account.
        </div>
      </div>
    </section>

    <section class="guide-step">
      <div class="guide-step-number">3</div>
      <div class="guide-step-content">
        <h2>Verify your registration</h2>
        <p>Apple will send verification codes to the email address and phone number you provided.</p>
        <p>Enter the codes and complete the account creation process.</p>
        <p>If the email does not arrive, check your <strong>Spam</strong> folder.</p>
      </div>
    </section>

    <section class="guide-step">
      <div class="guide-step-number">4</div>
      <div class="guide-step-content">
        <h2>Sign in to the new account in the App Store</h2>
        <p>Open the <strong>App Store</strong> and tap your profile icon.</p>
        <p>Scroll down and tap <strong>“Sign Out”</strong>.</p>
        <div class="guide-warning">
          <strong>Important:</strong> sign out only from the account inside the <strong>App Store</strong>. You do not need to sign out of your main iCloud account in your iPhone or iPad settings.
        </div>
        <p>After signing out:</p>
        <ol class="guide-simple-list">
          <li>Tap <strong>“Sign In With Apple Account”</strong>.</li>
          <li>Choose the option to sign in with another account.</li>
          <li>Enter the credentials for the account you created.</li>
        </ol>
      </div>
    </section>

    <section class="guide-step">
      <div class="guide-step-number">5</div>
      <div class="guide-step-content">
        <h2>Install the app</h2>
        <p>After you sign in, the App Store will show apps from the region of the new account.</p>
        <p>Find Happ or INCY, make sure the correct developer is listed, and install the app.</p>
      </div>
    </section>

    <div class="guide-success">
      <strong>Done!</strong> You can use the additional account to install and update apps that are unavailable in the Russian App Store. You can continue using your main iCloud account as usual.
    </div>

    <div class="guide-note">
      <strong>Continue installation</strong>
      <p>
        <a href="{{ site.baseurl }}/en/knowledge/install/ios/">
          Return to the Slon Privacy iOS installation guide →
        </a>
      </p>
    </div>

    <div class="guide-note">
      <strong>Recommendation</strong>
      <p>Use the additional account only for the App Store. Do not move photos, contacts, backups, or other data from your main account to it.</p>
    </div>

    <section class="guide-faq" aria-labelledby="new-account-faq-title">
      <h2 id="new-account-faq-title">Troubleshooting</h2>
      <details>
        <summary>The verification code does not arrive</summary>
        <p>Check the phone number and email address. Check your Spam folder and request the code again if necessary.</p>
        <p>If Apple offers verification by phone call, you can try that option instead of SMS.</p>
      </details>
      <details>
        <summary>Apple does not accept the phone number</summary>
        <p>Use another permanent phone number that you will continue to have access to, or try registering again later.</p>
      </details>
      <details>
        <summary>I cannot create an account</summary>
        <p>Try your browser's private mode, another email address, or a different Internet connection.</p>
      </details>
      <details>
        <summary>The app I need is not available after signing in</summary>
        <p>Check the region of the account you created and make sure the app is available in the App Store for the selected country.</p>
      </details>
    </section>
  </section>
</div>

<script>
document.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
  const trigger = event.target.closest('[data-open-app-tab]');
  if (!trigger) return;

  const panelId = trigger.getAttribute('data-open-app-tab');
  const panel = document.getElementById(panelId);
  if (!panel) return;

  const tabsRoot = trigger.closest('[data-app-tabs]');
  if (!tabsRoot) return;

  tabsRoot.querySelectorAll('[role="tab"]').forEach(function (button) {
    const isTarget = button.getAttribute('aria-controls') === panelId;
    button.setAttribute('aria-selected', isTarget ? 'true' : 'false');
  });

  tabsRoot.querySelectorAll('[role="tabpanel"]').forEach(function (item) {
    item.classList.toggle('is-active', item.id === panelId);
  });

  panel.scrollIntoView({ behavior: 'smooth', block: 'start' });
});
</script>
