<p><strong>Give a Slon Privacy subscription to friends, family, or colleagues. After payment, you will receive a gift code that you can send to the recipient in any convenient way.</strong></p>

<div class="guide-note">
<strong>Before you start</strong>
<p>Make sure you are signed in to your Slon Privacy account.</p>
</div>

<section class="guide-video-section" aria-labelledby="video-title">
  <h2 id="video-title">Video guide</h2>
  <p>Watch a short video guide on purchasing a gift subscription.</p>

  <video class="guide-video" controls playsinline preload="metadata">
    <source src="{{ site.baseurl }}/assets/video/knowledge-base/subscription/gift-subscription.mp4" type="video/mp4">
    Your browser does not support video playback.
  </video>
</section>

<section class="guide-step">
<div class="guide-step-number">1</div>
<div class="guide-step-content">
<h2>Go to the “Gift” section</h2>
<p>In your account, open the <strong>“Gift”</strong> section and click <strong>“Buy”</strong>.</p>
</div>
</section>

<section class="guide-step">
<div class="guide-step-number">2</div>
<div class="guide-step-content">
<h2>Choose a subscription period</h2>
<p>Select the duration of the gift subscription.</p>
<p><strong>The longer the subscription period, the lower the monthly cost.</strong></p>
</div>
</section>

<section class="guide-step">
<div class="guide-step-number">3</div>
<div class="guide-step-content">
<h2>Pay for the gift</h2>
<ul class="guide-simple-list">
<li>From your account balance, if sufficient funds are available;</li>
<li>SBP (Faster Payments System);</li>
<li>Bank card;</li>
<li>Tribute.</li>
</ul>
<p>Once the payment is successful, a gift code will be generated automatically.</p>
</div>
</section>

<section class="guide-step">
<div class="guide-step-number">4</div>
<div class="guide-step-content">
<h2>Send the gift code</h2>
<p>Copy the generated code and send it to the person you want to give the subscription to.</p>
</div>
</section>

<div class="guide-success">
<strong>Done!</strong> The recipient only needs to activate the gift code in their Slon Privacy account.
</div>
