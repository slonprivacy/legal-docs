<section class="app-tab-panel is-active" aria-labelledby="referral-title">
  <p><strong>Invite friends and others to Slon Privacy and earn 30% from every balance top-up made by your referrals.</strong></p>

  <section class="guide-step">
    <div class="guide-step-number">1</div>
    <div class="guide-step-content">
      <h2 id="referral-title">Invite a user</h2>
      <p>You can get your personal referral link in two ways from your account:</p>
      <ul class="guide-simple-list">
        <li>through the <strong>Slon Privacy Telegram bot</strong>;</li>
        <li>through your <strong>Slon Privacy account</strong>.</li>
      </ul>
      <img class="guide-image" src="{{ site.baseurl }}/assets/img/knowledge-base/referral/01-referral-links.webp" alt="Referral links to the Slon Privacy Telegram bot and account" loading="lazy">
      <p>Send your referral link to the person you want to invite using any convenient method. Once they register through your link, they will become your referral.</p>
    </div>
  </section>

  <section class="guide-step">
    <div class="guide-step-number">2</div>
    <div class="guide-step-content">
      <h2>Earn 30% from top-ups</h2>
      <p>You receive a <strong>30% commission from every balance top-up</strong> made by a user you invited.</p>
      <p>The commission is credited to your balance automatically.</p>
    </div>
  </section>

  <section class="guide-step">
    <div class="guide-step-number">3</div>
    <div class="guide-step-content">
      <h2>Use your earnings</h2>
      <p>Funds earned through the referral program can be used within Slon Privacy to:</p>
      <ul class="guide-simple-list">
        <li>pay for <strong>your own subscription</strong>;</li>
        <li>buy <strong>gift subscriptions</strong>.</li>
      </ul>
      <p>Once your referral commission reaches <strong>3,000 RUB</strong>, you can withdraw the funds to your personal bank card.</p>
    </div>
  </section>
