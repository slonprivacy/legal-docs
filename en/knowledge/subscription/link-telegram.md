<section class="app-tab-panel is-active" aria-labelledby="link-account-title">

  <p><strong>Add a backup sign-in method so you can keep access to your Slon Privacy account even if Telegram or your email is temporarily unavailable.</strong></p>

  <div class="guide-note">
    <strong>Why should you do this?</strong>
    <p>Linking Telegram and Email gives you an additional way to sign in. If one sign-in method is blocked or temporarily unavailable, you can use the other.</p>
  </div>

  <section class="guide-step">
    <div class="guide-step-number">1</div>
    <div class="guide-step-content">
      <h2 id="link-account-title">Why link Telegram and Email?</h2>
      <p>To make sure you can always sign in to your Slon Privacy account and manage your subscription, we recommend linking <strong>both sign-in methods</strong> — Telegram and Email.</p>
      <ul class="guide-simple-list">
        <li>keep access to your account if one sign-in method becomes unavailable;</li>
        <li>restore access more quickly if you run into problems;</li>
        <li>manage your subscription from any device.</li>
      </ul>
      <div class="guide-warning">
        <strong>Recommended:</strong> if you registered through Telegram, link your Email. If you registered through Email, link Telegram.
      </div>
    </div>
  </section>

  <section class="guide-video-section" aria-labelledby="link-video-title">

  </section>

  <section class="guide-step">
    <div class="guide-step-number">2</div>
    <div class="guide-step-content">
      <h2>Link a second sign-in method</h2>
      <ul class="guide-simple-list">
        <li>Go to <strong>Profile → Linked accounts</strong>.</li>
        <li>Select an additional sign-in method — <strong>Telegram</strong> or <strong>Email</strong>.</li>
        <li>Confirm the sign-in using the selected method.</li>
        <li>Select your primary account and click <strong>“Merge accounts”</strong>.</li>
      </ul>
    </div>
  </section>

  <section class="guide-step">
    <div class="guide-step-number">3</div>
    <div class="guide-step-content">
      <h2>After linking</h2>
      <p>Once the accounts are linked successfully, you will be able to sign in using either Telegram or Email.</p>
      <p>If one sign-in method becomes temporarily unavailable, you can continue using the other without losing your subscription or account settings.</p>
    </div>
  </section>

  <div class="guide-success">
    <strong>Done!</strong> Telegram and Email are now linked to the same Slon Privacy account.
  </div>
