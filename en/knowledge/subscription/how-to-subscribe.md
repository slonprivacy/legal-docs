<section class="app-tab-panel is-active" aria-labelledby="subscribe-title">

  <p><strong>You can subscribe to Slon Privacy through your account on the website or via the Telegram bot. The process takes just a few minutes.</strong></p>

  <div class="guide-note">
    <strong>Payment methods</strong>
    <ul class="guide-simple-list">
      <li>Russian bank cards;</li>
      <li>SBP (Faster Payments System);</li>
      <li>YooMoney;</li>
      <li>Tribute.</li>
    </ul>
    <p><strong>Coming soon:</strong> cryptocurrency and international bank card payments.</p>
  </div>

  <section class="guide-step">
    <div class="guide-step-number">1</div>
    <div class="guide-step-content">
      <h2 id="subscribe-title">Open your account</h2>
      <p>Go to the <a href="https://my.slonprivacy.app" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Slon Privacy website</a> or open the <a href="https://t.me/slon_privacy_bot" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Slon Privacy Telegram bot</a>.</p>
    </div>
  </section>

  <section class="guide-step">
    <div class="guide-step-number">2</div>
    <div class="guide-step-content">
      <h2>Sign in</h2>
      <p>Sign in through the website, or tap <strong>“Account”</strong> or <strong>“Personal account”</strong> in the Telegram bot.</p>
    </div>
  </section>

  <section class="guide-step">
    <div class="guide-step-number">3</div>
    <div class="guide-step-content">
      <h2>Activate your free trial</h2>
      <p>If this is your first time using the service, activate the <strong>3-day free trial</strong>.</p>
    </div>
  </section>

  <section class="guide-step">
    <div class="guide-step-number">4</div>
    <div class="guide-step-content">
      <h2>Choose a plan</h2>
      <p>Open the <strong>“Subscription”</strong> section and choose the plan and subscription period that suit you.</p>
      <div class="guide-note"><strong>Longer subscription periods offer better value.</strong></div>
    </div>
  </section>

  <section class="guide-step">
    <div class="guide-step-number">5</div>
    <div class="guide-step-content">
      <h2>Add funds and activate your subscription</h2>
      <p>If your balance is insufficient, add funds using a convenient payment method and activate the selected plan.</p>
      <p>You can enable automatic renewal when paying so your subscription renews without interruption.</p>
    </div>
  </section>

  <div class="guide-success">
    <strong>Done!</strong> Your Slon Privacy subscription is active. You can now connect your devices and choose any available location.
  </div>

  <div class="guide-note">
    <strong>What's next?</strong>
    <p>Install Slon Privacy on your device:</p>

      <a href="{{ site.baseurl }}/en/knowledge/install/macos/">macOS</a>
    </p>
  </div>
</section>
