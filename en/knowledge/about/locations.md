<section class="guide-step">
  <div class="guide-step-number">1</div>
  <div class="guide-step-content">
    <h2 id="available-locations">Available locations</h2>

    <p>Slon Privacy offers several types of locations for different needs:</p>

    <ul class="guide-simple-list">
      <li><strong>RU → ND</strong> — lets you use both Russian and international services at the same time.</li>
      <li><strong>ND</strong> — servers in the Netherlands.</li>
      <li><strong>DE</strong> — servers in Germany.</li>
      <li><strong>UK</strong> — servers in the United Kingdom.</li>
      <li><strong>RU</strong> — designed for accessing Russian services from abroad.</li>
    </ul>

    <div class="guide-note">
      <p><strong>Please note:</strong> the list of locations is approximate. The selection and number of available servers may change depending on server availability and status.</p>
    </div>
  </div>
</section>

<section class="guide-step">
  <div class="guide-step-number">2</div>
  <div class="guide-step-content">
    <h2 id="choose-location">Which location should you choose?</h2>

    <p>For everyday use, you can choose any suitable international location.</p>

    <p>If you need access to both Russian and international services, try <strong>RU → ND</strong>. If you are abroad and need access to Russian services, use <strong>RU</strong>.</p>

    <p>If the selected location is unstable or a service you need does not open, try another location.</p>
  </div>

</section>
